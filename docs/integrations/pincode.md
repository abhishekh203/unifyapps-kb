# Pincode connector

Source: https://www.unifyapps.com/docs/unify-integrations/pincode
Section: integrations

---

Pincode helps offline retailers and local shops modernize their operations by offering digital tools for inventory, orders, billing, and sourcing in one platform. It empowers merchants to digitize their business workflows, improve operational efficiency, and expand their market reach with real-time insights and integrated solutions. By streamlining backend processes and supporting local commerce, Pincode enables smarter decision-making and growth for store owners across India.

## Authentication:

Integrating your application with Pincode enables seamless integration of retail operations, real-time order and inventory synchronization, and improved business efficiency through unified digital commerce capabilities. Before starting, ensure you have the following information:

1. `Connection Name`**:** Choose a meaningful name for your connection. This name helps you identify the connection within your application or integration settings. It could be something descriptive like "MyAppPincodeIntegration".
2. `Connection type`: Select the connection type you would like to proceed with.
3. `Client ID`: Enter the Client ID associated with your Pincode account.
4. `Client secret`: Enter the Client secret associated with your Pincode account.

## ACTIONS :

| **Action Name** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| `Create item` | Create items in Pincode |
| `Update order status` | Updates order status in Pincode |
| `Update inventory items` | Updates inventory items in Pincode |
