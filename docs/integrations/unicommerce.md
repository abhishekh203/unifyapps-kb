# Unicommerce connector

Source: https://www.unifyapps.com/docs/unify-integrations/unicommerce
Section: integrations

---

Unicommerce helps businesses streamline and scale their e-commerce operations by centralizing order management, inventory tracking, warehouse operations, and multi-channel fulfillment in a unified platform. It enables seamless integration with online marketplaces, web stores, logistics partners, and ERP systems, ensuring accurate stock visibility and faster order processing.

## Authentication:

Integrating your application with Unicommerce allows you to synchronize orders, inventory levels, and fulfillment updates across marketplaces, web stores, and warehouse systems in real time. Before starting, ensure you have the following information:

1. `Connection Name`**:** Choose a meaningful name for your connection. This name helps you identify the connection within your application or integration settings. It could be something descriptive like "MyAppUnicommerceIntegration".
2. `Username`**:** Enter the username associated with your account.
3. `Password` **:** Enter the password for your account
4. `Tenant` **:** Enter the tenant information associated with your account.
