# Logik

Logik empowers teams to streamline complex product configuration, pricing, and quoting (CPQ) by centralizing rules, inventory, and logic in a headless, scalable platform. It delivers flexible customization and real-time guided selling to match intricate business requirements and accelerate sales cycles. With API-driven integrations, robust security, and seamless compatibility with CRM systems like SugarCRM or ServiceNow, Logik.io boosts collaboration and data-driven decisions for manufacturing and enterprise teams.

### Authentication:

Integrating your application with Logik enables seamless product configuration synchronization, accelerated quote generation, and optimized sales workflows. Before you begin, ensure you have the following information:

1. `Connection Name`: Choose a meaningful name for your connection. This name helps you identify the connection within your application or integration settings. It could be something descriptive like "MyAppLogikIntegration".
2. `Tenant` : Enter the tenant environment for your Logik application.
3. `Sector` : Enter the sector value assigned to your Logik application.
4. `API Key` : Enter the API Token from your Logik admin console.
