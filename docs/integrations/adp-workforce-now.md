# ADP Workforce Now integration | connect with UnifyApps

Source: https://www.unifyapps.com/docs/unify-integrations/adp-workforce-now
Section: integrations

---

Integrating your application with ADP Workforce Now enhances human resource and payroll management by enabling you to access employee data, manage payroll processes, and track workforce information within your workflows. ADP Workforce Now provides APIs to handle employee records, benefits, and payroll operations efficiently, helping organizations streamline HR processes and maintain accurate workforce data.

### **Authentication:**

Connecting your application to ADP Workforce Now enables efficient workforce operations by allowing you to manage employee information, payroll data, and HR activities seamlessly across your systems. Before you begin, ensure you have the following information:

- `Connection Name` : Choose a meaningful name for your connection. This name helps you identify the connection within your application or integration settings. It could be something descriptive like "MyAppADPWorkforceNowIntegration".
- `Sandbox` : Select ‘yes' if you are using a sandbox environment.
- `Client ID` : Enter the client ID provided by ADP Workforce Now for your application.
- `Client secret` : Enter the client secret provided by ADP Workforce Now for your application.
- `SSL Client Certificate` : Enter the SSL client certificate provided by ADP Workforce Now for your application.
- `SSL Client Key` : Enter the SSL client key provided by ADP Workforce Now for your application.
- `Custom CA Certificate` : The custom CA certificate provided by ADP Workforce Now for your application.
- `Unmask Sensitive Information` : Choose whether to unmask sensitive information in the logs.

## Actions :

| **Action Name** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| `Add personal contact` | Adds a personal contact in ADP Workforce Now |
| `Onboard applicant` | Onboards applicant to ADP Workforce Now |
| `Remove a personal contact` | Removes personal contact in ADP Workforce Now |
| `Get contact by ID` | Gets personal contact details by iD from ADP Workforce Now |
| `Get personal contacts` | Gets personal contacts of an associate from ADP Workforce Now |
| `Get worker by aoid` | Gets worker from ADP Workforce Now by aoid |
| `Get workers as of date` | Gets workers from ADP Workforce Now as of date |
| `Stop deduction instruction` | Stops a general deduction instruction for single workers in ADP Workforce Now |
| `Update personal contact` | Updates an existing personal contact in ADP Workforce Now |
